# Demo Sequence

For the final demonstration, use this sequence to tell the architecture story rather than simply showing screens.

1. **GS-01** — start with the nominal application; show structured facts → graph connections → active policy → context → AI draft → human gate → trace.
2. **GS-04** — prove entity resolution is not a trivial join by showing identity ambiguity.
3. **GS-11** — prove semantics matter by showing bank inflow vs tax turnover conflict.
4. **GS-12** — prove policy retrieval is version/authority aware, not similarity search.
5. **GS-08** — prove tenant filtering occurs before retrieval.
6. **GS-09** — prove documents are evidence, not instructions.
7. **GS-10** — disable AI assistance and continue manually.
8. **GS-13** — show human authority, adverse-factor evidence and recourse.
9. **GS-15** — close with outcome/feedback entering governed learning.
