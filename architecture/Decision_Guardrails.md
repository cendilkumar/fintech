# Decision Guardrails

These constraints survive every technology choice:

- AI has **no autonomous final credit authority**.
- Active credit policy is deterministic and versioned; generative output cannot modify it.
- Material AI-assisted factors require evidence/provenance.
- Tenant isolation is enforced before retrieval and display.
- Restricted evaluation attributes do not enter runtime decision context by default.
- Stale, missing and conflicting evidence remains visible.
- Untrusted uploaded/retrieved text cannot override instructions or policy.
- Sole-trader / natural-person-guarantor cases require explicit affected-person and governance analysis rather than sector-wide assumptions.
- Adverse/exception routes preserve human decision authority, material reason evidence and recourse workflow.
- Manual underwriting must remain available during AI outage.
- Decision traces record evidence, context, tool calls, policy checks, versions, concise rationale and human action—not hidden model chain-of-thought.
- Feedback cannot silently rewrite policy, ontology, semantic definitions or model configuration.
