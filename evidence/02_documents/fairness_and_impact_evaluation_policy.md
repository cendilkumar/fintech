# Fairness and Impact Evaluation Policy

**Status:** ACTIVE

Evaluation must distinguish legal-entity SMEs, sole traders and cases involving natural-person guarantors/co-borrowers. Segment diagnostics do not themselves define a legal fairness threshold. Any use of protected/sensitive attributes for evaluation requires explicit approved purpose, access and governance.

Historical approval/decline labels reflect prior human/policy behavior and selection effects. They must not be assumed to be objective ground truth for creditworthiness.
