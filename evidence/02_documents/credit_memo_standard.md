# Credit Memo Evidence Standard

**Status:** ACTIVE

A credit memo should separate:
1. verified source facts;
2. derived financial measures with calculation lineage;
3. active policy results;
4. unresolved discrepancies or missing evidence;
5. AI-assisted narrative explicitly marked as assistance;
6. human judgment and decision authority.

Every material AI-assisted factor must carry evidence references. Conflicting sources must be shown as conflict, not silently averaged away.
