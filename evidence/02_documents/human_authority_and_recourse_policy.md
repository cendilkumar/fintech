# Human Authority, Adverse Outcomes and Recourse

**Status:** ACTIVE

- `CREDIT_ANALYST` may prepare and recommend within assigned workflow scope but does not possess all final decision authority.
- `SENIOR_UNDERWRITER` handles designated exceptions and escalations.
- `CREDIT_AUTHORITY` is required where active policy routes the case to that role.
- The AI agent has no final credit authority.
- Adverse/conditional decisions must preserve the human decision, material reason codes/factors, source evidence and the case's approved recourse/appeal path.
- Human override is recorded; it does not automatically retrain a model or rewrite policy.
