# AI-Assisted Underwriting Decision-Support Policy

**Status:** ACTIVE

Permitted AI assistance may include document extraction, reconciliation support, evidence summarization, retrieval, discrepancy detection and draft memo generation.

The AI component must not:
- autonomously approve or decline credit;
- modify the active credit-policy bundle;
- invent missing evidence or policy thresholds;
- use restricted attributes as decision features without explicit approved purpose;
- bypass role/tenant controls;
- treat retrieved application text as system instructions;
- hide source freshness, conflicts or missing evidence.

A concise rationale may summarize observable factors. Hidden model chain-of-thought is neither required nor an audit artifact.
