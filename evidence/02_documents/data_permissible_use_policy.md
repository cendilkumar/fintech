# Data Permissible-Use and Purpose Policy

**Status:** ACTIVE

- Application documents: conditional and purpose-limited to the approved underwriting workflow.
- Commercial bureau: use requires verified contract/permissible purpose and provider freshness.
- Bank transaction data: restricted; consent/legal basis, minimization and retention controls apply.
- Tax/GST data: reuse/access basis must be verified for the exact workflow.
- Protected/sensitive attributes: restricted. Availability does not imply runtime decision permission.
- Historical memos: may support retrieval only when access, tenant, purpose and current-policy boundaries are enforced; past human decisions are not ground truth for creditworthiness.
