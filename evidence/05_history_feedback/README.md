# History, Outcomes and Feedback

History is useful for memory, evaluation and operational learning, but it has authority limits.

- Historical human decisions are records of what happened, not objective labels of creditworthiness.
- Acceptance/rejection of AI assistance is interaction feedback, not automatic training truth.
- Portfolio outcomes occur after the decision and can inform governed evaluation, but cannot retroactively justify every factor used.
- `restricted_fairness_eval_sample.csv` is evaluation-only synthetic data and must not enter runtime underwriting context.
