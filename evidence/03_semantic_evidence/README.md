# Semantic Evidence — Not a Completed Semantic Model

These files deliberately contain candidate mappings, conflicting terminology, identity clues and relationship clues. Participants must resolve the domain semantics rather than copying a finished ontology.

Critical distinctions include:
- organization vs natural person vs applicant role;
- bank inflow vs tax turnover vs financial-statement revenue;
- policy PASS vs final credit APPROVAL;
- OCR confidence vs decision confidence;
- historical decision vs evaluation ground truth;
- evidence present vs evidence usable/fresh/permitted.
